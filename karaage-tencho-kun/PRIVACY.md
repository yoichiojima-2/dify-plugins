# Privacy Policy

karaage-tencho-kun does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- All store data (sales, shifts, inventory, products) is fictional seed data bundled with the plugin and processed in an in-memory database. Nothing is persisted between sessions.
- The `hourly_weather` tool sends only geographic coordinates and dates to the public [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) weather API. No user identifiers or personal information are included in these requests.
- The plugin requires no credentials and connects to no other external services.
